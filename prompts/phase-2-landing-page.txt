SECTION 2 - HEADER
====================================================================

Items, in this order:

  Home | Courses | About | FAQ | Contact        [ REGISTER NOW ]

The nine policies do not appear in the header. They live in the footer
behind the policies index built in phase 1. A nine item header with an
eight item dropdown does not survive a phone screen.

There is no Instructors item. No trainer names exist yet.
There is no separate Registration item. It competes with the CTA.

Courses anchors to the courses section, which phase 3 fills. Until then,
either omit the Courses item or anchor it to a placeholder section with no
invented content. State which you chose and why.

REGISTER NOW is the only gold element on the page. Gold background, navy
label. Visible without scrolling on mobile.

Logo: the E mark alone at roughly 40px, with Edugistics set as text
beside it in the heading font. Do not put the stacked lockup in the
header.

Mobile: a standard disclosure menu. No animation beyond a plain open and
close. Keyboard accessible, focus trapped while open, closes on Escape.

SECTION 3 - HERO
====================================================================

Approved copy. Use verbatim. Change no wording.

  Empowering Teachers. Transforming Education.

  Edugistics brings over 25 years of educational experience in leadership
  to professional teacher development, offering practical training
  delivered by experienced trainers across core teaching subjects.

  Learn. Grow. Lead.

  [ Explore Courses ]   [ Register Now ]

Note two deliberate edits to the supplied copy, made because the original
claims are not supportable:
  "25 years of educational experience" became "25 years of educational
  experience in leadership", because Edugistics Ltd is a new company and
  the experience belongs to its founder.
  "expert instructors across all subjects" became "experienced trainers
  across core teaching subjects", because no trainer names or credentials
  exist on the site to support the stronger claim.

If Marwa rejects either edit she will say so. Do not revert them on your
own initiative.

Register Now is gold with a navy label. Explore Courses is a secondary
button, navy outline. Two buttons maximum.

No background image, no gradient, no video, no animation. Navy type on
white with generous space. The tagline "Your Educational Logistics
Partner" appears only inside the logo artwork, not as page copy.

SECTION 10 - PALETTE, TYPE AND PERFORMANCE
====================================================================

Palette and contrast rules are unchanged from phase 1 section 10.

Navy and whitespace carry the page. Four saturated colours at equal
weight reads as a children's app and destroys the consultancy
positioning. Gold appears on CTAs only. Coral appears nowhere in phase 2.

Two font families maximum. No gradients, no parallax, no scroll
animation, no carousels.

Serve resized logo assets. Never ship the 3947px original. Set explicit
width and height on every image. Lazy load everything below the fold.
Report Lighthouse performance and accessibility scores on mobile.

SEO metadata for the landing route: title, description, canonical, Open
Graph and Twitter tags. Use business facts only. Invent no claims.
