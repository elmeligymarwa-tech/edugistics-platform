==================================================
COLOUR SYSTEM, NON-NEGOTIABLE
==================================================

These four values are taken from the Edugistics logo. Use no colour outside this
palette and its tints, other than neutrals.

  Navy    #1E3060    dominant. Structure, headings, header, footer, body text.
  Teal    #3C8C90    secondary. Links, icons, section accents, the tagline.
  Gold    #E5B366    accent. Primary CTAs only, where the eye must land.
  Coral   #D8483C    sparing. Urgency and status only: seats remaining,
                     Registration Closed, form errors.

The hierarchy is not decorative preference, it mirrors the logo itself: the mark is
predominantly navy, the tagline is teal, gold and coral appear only inside the
letterform.

Do NOT distribute the four colours evenly across the page. Four saturated colours at
equal weight reads as a children's app and destroys the consultancy positioning. Navy
and generous white space carry the page. Teal appears regularly but quietly. Gold
appears rarely and always means "act here". Coral is almost absent.

Neutrals: white, and a small set of warm greys derived from navy at low opacity. No
pure black.

Check contrast. Gold on white fails for body text; if a gold CTA is used, the label
sits in navy, not white. Verify every text and background pair meets WCAG AA before
reporting the phase complete, and tell me any pair you had to adjust.

BRANDING

Use the existing Edugistics logo asset already in the project. Do not redraw,

educational consultancy, not a course marketplace.
