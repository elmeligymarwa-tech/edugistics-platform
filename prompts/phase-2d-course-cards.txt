SECTION D - DATES
====================================================================

Single-day course, isMultiDay false, sessions empty
  Show courseDate, formatted as the catalogue formats it.

Multi-day course, isMultiDay true
  Show the number of sessions and that the course runs across multiple
  dates. Do not show courseDate alone as if it were the whole course.
  The sessions array holds the real dates, ascending.

  Decide whether to list every date or show a count with a range, and
  state which. A four-session course listing four dates is honest; a
  twelve-session course listing twelve is a wall. Pick a rule that
  works at both and say what it is.

Never present a derived start date as though it were the only date.

====================================================================
SECTION E - STATUS
====================================================================

  isFull false
    No status label. The card is simply available.

  isFull true and waitlistEnabled true
    Show a "Waiting list" label. A full course with an open waitlist is
    materially different from one with places, and a teacher can still
    act on it.

  isFull true and waitlistEnabled false
    This course cannot be registered for and should not reach the
    landing page at all, since listPublicCourses excludes it. If you
    find such a course in the list, report it rather than designing
    around it.

The status label uses --edu-red as an accent. Do not use red anywhere
else in this phase. It carries meaning and must stay scarce.

SECTION F - LAYOUT AND VISUAL DESIGN
====================================================================

The availability sentence STAYS, above the cards. It sets the overall
context before a teacher evaluates individual courses. Do not remove it
and do not duplicate its figure on the cards.

Cards are navy and white with teal accents.
  White card surface on the section background.
  Course name in --edu-navy, League Spartan.
  Body text in --edu-navy, Source Sans 3.
  Teal for a rule, a border or a small icon. Not for body text.
  One gold Register button per card, navy label, linking to /training.

DO NOT put cards on a red background. White on --edu-red measures
3.77:1 and fails AA for text, and red carries status meaning.

The grid must work at one, three and twelve courses. A three-across
layout that looks deliberate at three and broken at four is a failure.
State your grid rule and how it behaves at 1, 2, 4 and 12.

Mobile first. Single column below the md breakpoint. Cards must not
force horizontal scroll at 320px.

CONTRAST, report every measured pair.
  --edu-navy on white          11.05:1  body, headings, links
  --edu-teal on white           3.67:1  FAILS AA for text. Rules,
    borders, icons and headings 24px and above only.
  --edu-red on white            3.77:1  FAILS AA for text. If the
    status label uses red text, it must be 24px and above or carry a
    non-colour cue as well. Report how you handled it.
  --edu-navy on --edu-gold      6.47:1  the Register button
  Every text pair must reach 4.5:1 or be justified as large text.
