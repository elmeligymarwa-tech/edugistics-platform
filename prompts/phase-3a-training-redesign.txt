SECTION D - MONTH GROUPING
====================================================================

Group the eligible courses by month.

  Group by the month of courseDate, the START month only. A course
  running from August into September appears once, under August. It does
  NOT appear again under September. This was decided deliberately: a
  teacher seeing the same course twice assumes there are two.

  Render only months that contain at least one course in the list you
  were given. No empty months, ever.

  Do not build a calendar. Do not generate a month range. Do not fetch
  anything. Derive the groups from the array already passed in.

  Order months chronologically, ascending, matching the order
  listPublicCourses already returns.

  Within a month, preserve the existing order. Do not re-sort.

  Past months: a course only reaches this list if
  isCourseOpenForRegistration allows it. If that puts a course in a
  month already past, it is still legitimately open and must be shown.
  Do not add a second filter of your own.

  Month heading format: state what you chose and why. It must be
  unambiguous about the year.
