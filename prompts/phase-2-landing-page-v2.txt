SECTION D - POSITIONING. THIS IS THE POINT OF THE PAGE.
====================================================================

Edugistics sells professional development directly to individual
teachers. The teacher is the customer, not a school, not a procurement
department.

A teacher landing on this page must understand within five seconds:
  who it is for          teachers and educators
  what it provides       professional development courses
  how to take part       browse, register, attend, get certified
  why Edugistics         experienced trainers, practical training

Language throughout uses "teachers", "your professional development",
"your teaching", "courses for teachers", "register for your course".

Do not position Edugistics as a school procurement service, a corporate
training provider, or an institutional consultancy. Do not imply a
school must buy a package before a teacher can train.

The wider consultancy and school-management offering may be mentioned as
a secondary capability in About. It must never lead.

Final test before you report complete: if a teacher who has never heard
of Edugistics reads only the top of this page on a phone, do they know
they can register themselves for training? If not, revise.

SECTION H - PALETTE, CONTRAST AND TYPE
====================================================================

Use the existing brand tokens through their Tailwind utility classes.
Hardcode no hex values. Do not edit globals.css.

Contrast rules, non negotiable:
  brand-teal on white is 3.9:1 and fails AA for body text. Never for
    inline links or text under 18.66px. Headings 24px and above, icons
    and borders only.
  Inline links use brand-navy with a visible underline.
  Gold on white fails for text. The gold CTA carries a navy label.
  Every text and background pair reaches 4.5:1.
  Report the measured ratio for every pair used.

Navy and whitespace carry the page. Gold appears on the header CTA only.
Coral appears nowhere in this phase. Four saturated colours at equal
weight reads as a children's app and destroys the consultancy
positioning.

Two font families maximum, preferring what is already loaded. No
gradients, no parallax, no scroll animation, no carousels.

Mobile first. Most teachers arrive from social media or WhatsApp on a
phone. The B2C message must be visible without scrolling.
