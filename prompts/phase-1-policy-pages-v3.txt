F.1 REFUND AND CANCELLATION (replacing the existing page)

  - Full refund when a teacher cancels seven or more days before the
    course start date.
  - Less than seven days before the start date, no refund.
  - Two alternatives remain up to three days before the start date:
    transfer to another Edugistics course, or nominate a substitute
    teacher.
  - Requests go by email to Info@edugistics.online, subject line
    "Teacher change request". A substitute must be named in that email.
  - Transfers may be made to any Edugistics course, not only a later run
    of the same one. No limit on the number of transfers. A transferred
    booking does not expire.
  - Where the new course costs more, the teacher pays the difference.
  - Where the new course costs less, and the transfer is requested seven
    or more days before the original start date, the difference is
    refunded.
  - Where the new course costs less, and the transfer is requested inside
    seven days of the original start date, the difference is held as
    credit, not refunded in cash.
  - Credit expires twelve months from the date it is issued. Credit is
    never convertible to cash. Credit stays with the teacher who paid and
    cannot be passed to a colleague.
  - The refund right attaches to the original booking. Once the original
    course passes its seven day mark, the booking stays transferable but
    carries no refund right.
  - Less than three days before the start date, non attendance is a no
    show. No refund, no transfer, no substitution.
  - Once a course has started, no refund, and no partial or pro-rata
    refund for sessions missed.
  - If Edugistics cancels for any reason, including low enrolment, the
    teacher chooses a full refund or a transfer to the rescheduled date.
    Minimum three days notice for low enrolment.
  - If Edugistics reschedules, the new date is announced before the
    original start date. A teacher who finds it inconvenient may request
    a full refund at any point up to the new start date.
  - Edugistics is not responsible for travel, accommodation or any other
    associated costs. KEEP THIS CLAUSE. It exists on the current page.
  - Refunds are issued in Egyptian Pounds for the amount paid. Where a
    card was issued outside Egypt the amount received may differ because
    of exchange rates applied by the issuer, and Edugistics does not
    cover the difference. KEEP THIS CLAUSE. It exists on the current page.
  - Approved refunds are processed by Edugistics within 48 hours. Bank
    transfer refunds arrive the same day. Card refunds appear according
    to the issuing bank's own processing times.
  - Edugistics deducts no administrative fee.
  - Refund requests go by email with the registration reference and the
    course name. Phone or WhatsApp requests are acknowledged, then
    confirmed by email before processing begins. KEEP THIS PROCESS.

  DELETE the current section 7 on recorded and downloadable materials.
  No session is recorded and no recordings are provided.

  DO NOT state any number of days for card refunds. That figure is not
  confirmed. No estimate, no range, no "typically", no "14 working days".

F.3 DIGITAL DELIVERY

  - Online courses: the confirmation email carries the Zoom joining link.

  - Online courses: materials are sent after the course begins.
